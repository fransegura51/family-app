# Anniversary / Family Celebration Specification

A flexible adult/family celebration based on the shared engine.

## Celebration subtypes
Examples:
- Anniversary
- Retirement
- Surprise party
- Family reunion
- Engagement / proposal celebration
- Graduation
- Special celebration
- Other

PEPA may recommend modules based on subtype; user remains in control.

## Anniversary-specific data
Optional:
- names of protagonists
- number of years being celebrated
- surprise flag

## Surprise safety
If marked as a surprise, use contextual warnings before actions that could accidentally expose the event to the honouree.
Do not auto-message people.

## Invitations
Use shared invitation editor and RSVP.
Adult template families include:
- elegant
- romantic
- modern
- party
- vintage
- floral
- minimalist
- humorous

## Activities
Optional ideas may include:
- speeches
- toast
- music
- family games
- presentation of memories
- joint gift moment

Do not require shared photo albums/pages.

## Gifts received
May be enabled optionally but is off by default.

## Venue logic
If restaurant/venue provides most services, recommend hiding redundant menu, shopping, decoration or activities modules.
