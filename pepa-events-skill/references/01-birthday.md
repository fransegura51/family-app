# Birthday Specification

## Creation fields
- Event name: required.
- Person having birthday: optional; may reference a PEPA family member or manual name.
- Age turning: REQUIRED.
- Date: optional at creation.
- Date status: pending / provisional / confirmed.
- Time: optional.
- Venue/type: optional.
- Budget: optional.
- Theme: optional.
- Do NOT ask approximate guest count; derive it from guest list.

## Venue types
Include common options such as:
- Home
- Indoor play centre / ball park
- Rented venue
- Restaurant
- Outdoors
- Other

A child’s birthday may begin planning before a venue is booked. Venue availability can determine the final date.
Support multiple candidate dates if useful, while keeping one confirmed date once settled.

## Recommended modules
Birthday may use:
- Guests
- Invitations / RSVP
- Preparations
- Budget
- Menu + shopping
- Decoration
- Activities / games
- Day plan
- PEPA conclusions

All optional except core event identity and age. Full-service venues may make food, decoration and activities unnecessary.

## Invitations
Birthday invitations must support all ages, not only children.
Template families should include:
- children’s themes
- teen styles
- elegant
- minimalist
- modern
- floral
- dinner/party
- milestone birthdays (18, 30, 40, 50, 60, etc.)
- humorous / surprise styles

For children, thematic support should be strong, but do not bundle unlicensed copyrighted TV/video-game characters as PEPA-owned assets.
Architecture should support:
- original/generic PEPA themes;
- user-supplied images where permitted;
- future licensed packs.

## Menu + shopping
User may ask PEPA to propose menu and quantities based on confirmed/expected adults and children, budget and constraints.
Generated shopping list can be transferred to PEPA Purchases after user confirmation.

## Decoration
PEPA suggests; family selects all/some/none.

## Activities
Especially valuable when venue does not include entertainment.
Suggestions may include age range, duration and required materials.
Materials can flow to Purchases after confirmation.

## Photos
Do NOT add a shared birthday photo album, public photo page, or post-event photo-sharing feature as part of Birthday scope.

## End of event
Archive + summary + duplicate for next year.
